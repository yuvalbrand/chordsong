Privacy Policy

Chordsong · Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: Chordsong collects no personal data. Your songs, scans and settings stay on your device.

What the app stores

The chord progressions you enter, the settings you choose for them, and any chord-sheet photos you scan are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a chord sheet, and photos or files are read only when you choose to import them. Text recognition runs on your device; images are never uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play on Android. We don't receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

On Android

On Android, Chordsong reads chord sheets on your device with Google's ML Kit, part of Google Play services. To keep it working, ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic information, such as the device model, the app version and how long text recognition took. It never includes your images, scans or songs, and it isn't used to identify you. Google handles it under its privacy policy; see also ML Kit's data disclosure.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising of our own
  • No data sharing or selling

Children

Chordsong is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
