Chordsong Support

You know the chords. Chordsong shows you how to make them sound good.

Need help or have an idea? Email chordsong.app@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a song?
Tap + and type the chords (for example C G Am F), or scan a printed chord sheet with the camera, or import a photo or PDF.

The scan missed or misread a chord.
Every scan opens a review screen with the original page next to the chords that were found. Fix anything there before saving. Scanning reads chord symbols only, not sheet-music notation.

Which chords are supported?
Major, minor, diminished, augmented, sus2, sus4, 6, 7, maj7, m7, m7♭5, dim7, add9, 9, maj9, m9 and slash chords such as D/F♯. Common spellings work too: Am, A-, Amin; Cmaj7, CM7, CΔ7.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?
Open the ⓘ About screen and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?
In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Chordsong. You can also tap Manage Subscription in the app's About screen.
