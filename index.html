Chordsong

You know the chords.
We show you how to make them sound good.

Chordsong turns any chord progression into a real piano accompaniment, and shows exactly what each hand plays.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone & iPad

See what each hand plays

Keys light up as the piano plays, with a finger number on every one. A rhythm grid shows when each hand comes in.

  • Pop
  • Ballad
  • Arpeggio
  • Waltz
  • Jazz

Beginner to Advanced, from simple triads to jazz shell and rootless voicings and a walking bass.

Play along your way

Listen first. Then take the left or the right hand yourself while Chordsong plays the other.

In Free play, the app keeps the left hand going and the right-hand keys show the notes that fit each chord, so you can improvise without hitting a wrong one.

Bring your own songs

Type the chords the way you'd write them (C G Am F, Dm7 G7 Cmaj7, D/F♯) or scan a printed chord sheet with the camera.

Set how long each chord lasts, change the key and the tempo, and loop the hard part.

Print it as sheet music

Every arrangement becomes a grand-staff score with chord names, ready to print or share as PDF or MusicXML.

Yours, and private

Your songs and scans stay on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads.
